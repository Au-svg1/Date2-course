# Instructor Overview: Three-Hour Date2 Competition

Seven teams build different research-data products from the same Date2 source. Students begin with `START_HERE.md`; live challenges remain instructor-only in `live_challenges.md`.

Suggested pacing: 25 minutes orientation and data inspection, 55 minutes rule design, 50 minutes product construction, 30 minutes peer review/revision, and 20 minutes oral defense/live challenges. Assess reasoning, reproducibility, provenance, AI verification, peer-review response, and defense—not code length.

Group 6 provides the conceptual bridge: Date2 ends with **building a trustworthy analysis-ready dataset**, while Date3 begins with **discovering patterns from that dataset**. Do not ask Group 6 to run clustering or Random Forest during this activity.
