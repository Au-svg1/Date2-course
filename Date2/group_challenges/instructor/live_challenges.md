# Live Oral-Defense Challenges — Instructor Only

Reveal one challenge only after the team has presented its original assumptions.

1. **Group 1:** Relax your station-coverage rule. Which stations enter the dataset, and what changes?
2. **Group 2:** Remove the lowest-coverage weeks. Does the apparent station ranking change?
3. **Group 3:** Change your comparability definition. Which station pairs are no longer comparable?
4. **Group 4:** Two stations have the same OverallAvailability but different Continuity. Which is better for event analysis, and why?
5. **Group 5:** Apply your strict and inclusive quality rules. Which conclusion changes most?
6. **Group 6:** Add one more required variable. How much sample size and station coverage are lost?
7. **Group 7:** Give the team one randomly selected final record and ask them to trace it back to its source using only their submitted workflow.

Score the quality of adaptation and explanation, not whether the team preserves its original conclusion.
