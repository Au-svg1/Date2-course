# START HERE — Dissolved Oxygen Data Product

## Mission

Build a defensible dissolved-oxygen data product for temporal and cross-station exploration.

Open `challenge_brief.md` first, then inspect `data/` with `starter_analysis.py`. The starter only loads and displays the data; your team must design the analysis.

## Submit

Place the final data product, runnable analysis, data dictionary, decision log, provenance statement, and completed AI verification log in `submission_template/`. See `submission_template/expected_outputs.md` for details.

## AI use

AI may help with code and critique, but every transformation and conclusion must be checked against the supplied data and defended by the team. Do not upload course data to an external service unless the instructor has explicitly authorized it.

## Assessment

The competition assesses scientific judgment, reproducibility, metadata/provenance, transparent AI verification, peer-review quality, and oral defense. Code complexity is not rewarded by itself.
